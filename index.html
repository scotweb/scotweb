About ScotWeb

Built in Glasgow.
Crafted for the world.

ScotWeb is an independent studio working at the intersection of design, engineering and craft. We're based in the shadow of the Glasgow School of Art — and it shows: every project leans on the same instinct for pattern, restraint and hand-finished detail that put this city on the design map a century ago.


Just as the Clyde once carried Glasgow's ambitions out to the world, "Clyde-built" became shorthand for something made properly. We hold our own work to the same mark — considered in every detail, from the first sketch to the final pixel, built to last.
